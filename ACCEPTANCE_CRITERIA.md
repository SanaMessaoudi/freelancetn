# FreelanceTN — Acceptance Criteria

## US01 — Freelancer Registration

- The freelancer can enter their full name.
- The freelancer can enter a valid email address.
- The freelancer can create a password.
- The system rejects an email that is already registered.
- The account is created when all information is valid.

## US02 — Company Registration

- The company can enter its name.
- The company can enter a valid email.
- The company can provide its website.
- The system creates the company account when all required information is valid.

## US03 — Login

- The user can enter their email and password.
- The system accepts valid credentials.
- The system displays an error for incorrect credentials.
- The user accesses the correct account according to their role.

## US04 — Logout

- The user can click the logout button.
- The system ends the user's session.
- The user is redirected to the login page.

## US05 — Create Freelancer Profile

- The freelancer can add a professional description.
- The freelancer can add skills.
- The freelancer can add work experience.
- The freelancer can save and update their profile.

## US06 — Upload CV

- The freelancer can upload a CV in PDF format.
- The system rejects unsupported file formats.
- The uploaded CV can be viewed by the freelancer.

## US07 — Publish Job

- The company can enter a job title.
- The company can enter a job description.
- The company can specify required skills.
- The company can publish the job offer.
- The published offer appears in the job list.

## US08 — Search Jobs

- The freelancer can search by job title.
- The freelancer can filter jobs by required skills.
- The system displays matching job offers.
- The system displays a message when no jobs match.

## US09 — Apply for a Job

- The freelancer can open a job offer.
- The freelancer can submit an application.
- The system confirms successful submission.
- The freelancer can view their application status.

## US10 — Review Applications

- The company can view applications for its job offers.
- The company can view freelancer profiles and CVs.
- The company can accept or reject an application.
- The application status is updated.
