# FreelanceTN — Product Backlog

## 1. Product Vision

FreelanceTN is an online platform that connects Tunisian freelancers with international clients and companies looking for remote professional services.

The platform aims to help Tunisian talent access global job opportunities while allowing international companies to discover, evaluate, and collaborate with qualified Tunisian professionals.

## 2. Target Users

### Freelancer
A Tunisian professional who wants to:
- Find international remote job opportunities.
- Create a professional profile.
- Showcase skills and experience.
- Upload a CV and portfolio.
- Apply for job offers.
- Track application status.

### Client / International Company
A foreign individual or company who wants to:
- Create an account.
- Publish job opportunities.
- Search for Tunisian freelancers.
- View freelancer profiles and portfolios.
- Receive and review applications.
- Select suitable candidates.

### Administrator
A platform manager who wants to:
- Manage freelancer and company accounts.
- Verify company profiles.
- Monitor job publications.
- Manage reported content.
- Maintain platform security.

## 3. Main Product Features

### Epic 1 — Authentication and Account Management
- User registration.
- User login and logout.
- Password recovery.
- Role-based access control.

### Epic 2 — Freelancer Management
- Create and update freelancer profiles.
- Add professional skills and experience.
- Upload CV.
- Add portfolio projects.
- Manage profile visibility.

### Epic 3 — Company Management
- Company registration.
- Create and update company profiles.
- Company verification.
- Manage company information.

### Epic 4 — Job Management
- Publish job offers.
- Edit and delete job offers.
- Display available opportunities.
- Search and filter jobs.
- View job details.

### Epic 5 — Application Management
- Apply for job offers.
- Submit a CV and application message.
- View received applications.
- Accept or reject applications.
- Track application status.

### Epic 6 — Administration
- Manage users.
- Verify companies.
- Monitor job offers.
- Handle reports and complaints.

## 4. Product Goal

Build a secure, reliable, and user-friendly platform that facilitates professional collaboration between Tunisian freelancers and international clients.

## 5. Initial Development Priority

The first development phase will focus on:
1. Authentication.
2. Freelancer profiles.
3. Company profiles.
4. Job publication.
5. Job applications.
